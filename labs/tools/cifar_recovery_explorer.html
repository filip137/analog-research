Metrics, controls, costs and limits

Accuracy uses the full 10,000-image test set. Teacher KL is KL(original digital teacher || student), in nats at temperature 1. Lower KL is favorable. Teacher accuracy is 93.53% on CIFAR-10 and 70.16% on CIFAR-100. Three array draws are not three HWA training seeds.

Depth is the last four/eight analog convolutions plus classifier, with a frozen digital prefix. Every recovery epoch processes 45,000 images; five epochs are 3,520 minibatch updates. Calibration trains only digital gains, suffix BN affine parameters and classifier bias. Joint recovery also updates physical weights.

Sources: standard HWA targets nominal programming; noise HWA is a generic robustness source; CDT is corruption-aware HWA trained on the specified fault type and mixed 2/3/5% rates. CDT covers nominal and its own fault type only. Thirty-one of 40 selected HWA fits retain a late-convergence flag. Missing combinations were not run; no test-based best-source selection is made here.

Costs: PCM weight updates perform 3,520 full Gaussian endpoint reprograms. OM uses pulse updates; the historical sweep has a 640-pulse-per-cell recovery cap, while the later open-loop and LR campaigns are uncapped in total but retain one pulse per update. Open-loop recovery performs zero verification reads. These counts are not energy estimates.
