## Question

Does verifying and rewriting devices outside ±0.02 of the target reduce the
RMS error one hour after programming?

## Hypothesis

At least one retry arm lowers `rms_err["3600"].mean` by 10% or more relative to
`baseline`.

## Decision rule (fixed before data)

For each retry arm, `rel = 1 - rms_err["3600"].mean(arm) / rms_err["3600"].mean(baseline)`.

- **supported:** some retry arm has `rel >= 0.10` and `extra_writes_per_device.mean <= 0.5`.
- **contradicted:** every retry arm has `rel < 0.05`.
- **inconclusive:** otherwise.

The rule uses means over 3 seeds; report the seed standard deviations alongside.

## Outcomes

- Supported: retrying is worth its write cost for 1 h retention.
- Contradicted: programming error is not what limits 1 h error; look at drift.
- Inconclusive: retrying helps a little; its value depends on the write budget.
