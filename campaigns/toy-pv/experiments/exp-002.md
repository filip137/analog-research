## Question

exp-001 showed that verify alone barely moves the 1 h error. If pre-compensation is paired with a verify scheme that costs at most 0.5 extra writes, can it lower rms_err["3600"] substantially without first-read error going above baseline? Roughly where along t_ref does the first-read limit bind?

## Hypothesis

At least one arm with max_retries 3, tol 0.03 and pre-compensation qualifies, meaning it spends at most 0.5 extra writes per device and its first-read error is no worse than baseline, and it lowers rms_err["3600"].mean by 15% or more relative to baseline. The best such arm is expected at t_ref of about 10 s; t_ref 20 s and above are expected to break the first-read limit.

## Decision rule (fixed before data)

All quantities are means over the 5 seeds. Report seed SDs and the per-seed paired differences against baseline.

Definitions for each arm A:
- `rel(A) = 1 - rms_err["3600"].mean(A) / rms_err["3600"].mean(baseline)`
- **qualifying(A)**: `extra_writes_per_device.mean(A) <= 0.5` AND `rms_err["1"].mean(A) <= rms_err["1"].mean(baseline)`

The precomp candidates are the arms v3t03_pc3, v3t03_pc10, v3t03_pc20 and v3t03_pc3600.

- **supported:** some qualifying precomp candidate has `rel >= 0.15`.
- **contradicted:** v3t03 (no precomp) has `extra_writes_per_device.mean <= 0.5`, and no qualifying precomp candidate has `rel >= 0.05`. This covers both cases: every candidate that cuts the 1 h error breaks the first-read limit, or the qualifying ones gain less than 5%.
- **inconclusive:** otherwise. This includes the best qualifying candidate having 0.05 <= rel < 0.15, and v3t03 itself going over 0.5 extra writes, which would make it a design failure rather than evidence against pre-compensation.

Two diagnostics are reported but do not affect the verdict:
1. `rel(pc3600)`. A value of 0.5 or more confirms that drift bias, not write noise, dominates the 1 h error.
2. For any arm whose rms_err["1"] mean is within 2% of baseline's, its qualifying status is flagged as borderline. In that case, give the per-seed paired sign of `rms_err["1"](A) - rms_err["1"](baseline)`.

## Outcomes

- **Supported:** the best scheme is verify within the write budget plus mild pre-compensation. exp-003 should narrow t_ref between the largest qualifying value and the smallest non-qualifying one (for example 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 s), with tol near 0.029–0.03, to find the 1 h optimum under the first-read limit. A confirmation run or a stop can follow.
- **Contradicted:** under the first-read limit, pre-compensation cannot help in this toy. The answer becomes verify-only within budget (v3t03 if it beats exp-001's retry1, otherwise retry1). The campaign can stop after at most one tol check. The pc3600 and v3t03_pc3600 arms then show what the first-read limit costs.
- **Inconclusive:** pre-compensation helps modestly within the limits, or the verify arm went over budget. exp-003 should retune tol and max_retries to free more room at first read, then retest t_ref in 5–15 s.
