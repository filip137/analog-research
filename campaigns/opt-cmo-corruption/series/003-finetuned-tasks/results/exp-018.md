# Result: exp-018 (partial, SST-2)

**On SST-2, on-chip learning after the verify rewrite does not improve task
accuracy for the fine-tuned teacher.** At every fault case the best learning
row is within about one SE of the best no-learning row. The pretrained control
is at chance on SST-2, so the main question ("larger gain when fine-tuned") is
inconclusive on accuracy. On choice KL, learning combined with calibration
removes a somewhat larger share of the remaining residual for the fine-tuned
teacher (23–25%) than for the pretrained one (13–19%).

The user redirected the scope to [exp-019](../experiments/exp-019-analog-lora.md)
on October 15. HellaSwag and PIQA therefore have selected FT teachers but no FT
crossbar runs.

**Main-question statistic:** D = Δ_FT − Δ_PT is −0.11 ± 0.45 pt (random 3%)
and −0.57 ± 0.50 pt (gmax 3%). Inconclusive by rule, because PT is near chance.

## Interpretation

**Observations:**
- **FT is robust to these faults.** For the fine-tuned SST-2 model, 3% stuck
  devices cost at most about 1.8 pt raw (gmax), and the verify rewrite alone
  recovers to within about 0.1 pt of clean.
- **No accuracy room.** There is nothing left for on-chip learning to recover in
  accuracy. All Δ values are below 0.5 pt and within 1.2 SE, so they are
  inconclusive, not harmful.
- **Weight learning alone hurts.** R3 worsens choice KL in every case and for
  both teachers, consistent with exp-013/016/017.
- **Gains need calibration.** Learning helps on KL only together with digital
  calibration (R4 vs R2).

**Inference (limited):** the hypothesis that fine-tuned models leave more room
for on-chip training is not supported on SST-2. The task's decision margin is
large, so faults barely move accuracy. The KL-residual difference between FT
and PT is small and comes from a single array. HellaSwag and PIQA, whose
margins are smaller, were not run under this design.
