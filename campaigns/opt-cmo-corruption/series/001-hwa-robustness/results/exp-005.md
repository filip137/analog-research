## Readiness measurements

Both schedule smokes run the same full batch of 32 and analog/digital parameter
scope as the scientific candidates. Their shorter warm-up and decay horizons
are explicitly declared smoke settings.

19.71 GB peak allocated GPU memory. Preparation used 3.77 seconds and the CMO
device stage 1.25 seconds. Retained stage artifacts total approximately 0.918 GB.

Final KL was 1.05161 for step decay and 1.09219 for linear decay, with finite
healthy and 3%-fault components. These values establish
execution, not a preferred scientific LR or schedule.

The measured update/selection cadence projects to about 50–60 minutes per full
candidate, within the frozen 90-minute allowance. The shared campaign cap is
16 GPU-hours, including smokes, failed attempts and any authorized operational
replay, with 30 GB incremental storage and a 12-hour execution window.
