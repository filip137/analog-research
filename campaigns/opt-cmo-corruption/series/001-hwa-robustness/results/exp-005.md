## Scope and learning-rate interpretation, 2026-10-11

The user's concern prompted a read-only check of the saved resolved configs,
fit records and optimizer implementation. All ten focused LR/schedule candidates
and both duration candidates trained **whole-model scope**, with two optimizer
groups receiving the same LR. Thus the chosen peak did not come from an
analog-only sweep. The current whole-model cosine source likewise records
base rates `[0.005, 0.005]` and final rates `[0.001, 0.001]`, including
96,895,488 additional digital parameters. The analog/calibration group uses
normalized analog q coordinates; the additional digital weights use their
ordinary coordinates. Equal numerical rates do not establish suitable rates
for both groups.

The unresolved experimental limitation is that development tied these two rates
together. It never tested the current analog rate with a smaller additional
digital rate. Moreover, selection minimized mean healthy/faulted KL on synthetic
development text, accepting the healthy-performance cost shown above; the final
cosine schedule was a new pragmatic choice rather than a tested sweep winner.

The saved standard-3% cosine histories show a substantial early disturbance:
healthy development KL starts at 0.021307 for both scopes, then reaches 2.063880
for whole-model versus 0.128593 for analog at update 512. At the selected sources,
clean development KL is 0.260614 versus 0.019190. These observations motivate
testing whether the digital rate is too aggressive, but do not isolate its
causal contribution from training scope or establish an implementation defect.

The conclusion remains restricted to this shared schedule; it does not establish
that a suitably tuned whole-model method is worse. A focused next test would
hold the analog/calibration schedule fixed and compare smaller digital rates
against the existing frozen-digital and equal-rate endpoints, using development
data and a declared healthy-performance criterion. Any new criterion or sweep
is prospective and requires a new scientific decision; no runs or frozen rules
were changed by this audit.
