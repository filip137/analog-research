## Interpretation

**Observations.**
- On the WikiText-2 test, HWA distilled on generated text costs much more
  fidelity on healthy and lightly faulted hardware than HWA distilled on
  WikiText-2 itself:
  - standard HWA at 0 %: perplexity 54.9 against 47.9;
  - corruption-aware HWA at 0 %: 59.2 against 49.0.
- Within the generated-text regime, Experiment 1's qualitative picture
  holds. HWA is essential at 1–5 %, and corruption-aware HWA trades
  healthy-hardware fidelity for robustness. The crossover moves from 2–3 %
  to 4 %.
- Neither change you asked for buys a material improvement:
  - doubling the Gaussian noise moves the trade-off slightly toward
    robustness, but stays under 10 % at every rate;
  - overestimating the training fault rate (5 % instead of 3 %) only costs:
    13 % at 0 % and nothing gained at 4–5 %.
- **Overfitting is reduced.** The development/training ratio grows less than
  in Experiment 1 (1.08–1.20 → 1.09–1.48, against 1.21–1.26 → 1.63).

**Inference and limits.**
- **The data comparison is confounded by domain.** The WikiText-2 test is
  in-distribution for the Experiment 1 sources and out-of-distribution for
  these ones. A large part of "Experiment 1 is better" is likely Experiment 1
  specializing the analog MLPs to Wikipedia text, which is exactly what its
  in-domain test rewards.
  - Settling this needs the symmetric 2×2: each training corpus × each test
    domain.
  - That means also evaluating the Experiment 1 sources on held-out
    generated text (`test_100k.txt`, 390 sequences, unused so far).
  - It needs a contract extension: deployments verify that a source's data
    digest matches their own, so a source cannot be scored on another
    corpus's test set yet.
- **The generated text is not the teacher's own distribution:** temperature
  0.9 and top-k 50, with heavy boilerplate repetition. Pure-sampled text
  (as in Analog Foundation Models) might transfer better. This is untested.
- **The comparisons are narrow.** Each source is one training run with a
  fixed 50-epoch budget, still improving, at one learning rate (5e-3) tuned
  on Experiment 1. Experiment 1c-D estimates training-run variance and
  Experiment 1c-B tests a decayed learning rate.
- **Evidence class:** exploratory and model-based.

## Decision

**Settled within this regime:**
- Keep 3 % noise. 6 % is not materially better.
- Do not overestimate the training fault rate: 5 % only costs.
- HWA stays essential, and the pretrained model is not a deployment source
  above 0 %.

**Pending your decision: which training corpus HWA should use.** The
WikiText-2 test cannot decide this alone. Proposed next step:
- add an evaluation-only "transfer test" contract extension, scoring a
  frozen source on a declared second test cohort;
- evaluate the four Experiment 1 and Experiment 1b L6 sources on both test
  domains. That is about 8 Jean Zay dev jobs and no retraining.

Then choose a corpus by the out-of-domain row, or by a third, neutral corpus.
