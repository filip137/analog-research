## Interpretation

**Observations** (out of domain, generated-text training, WikiText-2 test):
1. **The learning-rate schedule matters more than any arm choice.** A tenfold
   decay for the last 10 epochs improves every source by 11–27 % wherever the
   source was not already at its robustness limit. The constant 5e-3 plateaus
   were mostly an optimizer noise floor.
2. **Generic Gaussian noise gives the best single source.** The 3 → 6 → 9 →
   12 % ladder trades healthy-hardware fidelity for fault robustness and
   saturates at about 9–12 %. With or without decay, 9 % noise is in the best
   set at every rate from 1 % to 5 %, and it is materially better than
   corruption-aware training at 0–1 %. The L3 replication agrees: 6 % noise
   is the best source at every rate from 1 % to 5 %.
3. **Corruption-aware variants add nothing material here:**
   - mixed rates do no better than a fixed 3 %;
   - a 5 % training rate only costs;
   - temporary stuck masks on top of 9 % noise do not beat 9 % noise.
4. **Single training runs resolve 10 % differences.** Run-to-run variance is
   at most about 6 % (standard) and 0.7 % (corruption-aware).

**Inference and limits.**
- **These rankings are out of domain.** In-domain, corruption-aware 3 % beats
  standard 3 % by 18–38 % at 3–5 % for both corpora ([exp-004](exp-004.md)).
  The noise ladder and decay were not evaluated in-domain, so it is untested
  whether 9 % noise also matches corruption-aware training in-domain.
- **Fixed budget.** 50 epochs; every source selected epoch 48–50. Decay was
  tested at one milestone and factor only.
- **Partial-model HWA (caveat, 2026-10-10).** Every HWA source here trained
  only the analog fc1/fc2 weights plus their biases, the LayerNorm in front of
  each analog MLP and per-matrix output gains: 11.3 % (L3) or 22.6 % (L6) of
  OPT-125M's 125.2 M parameters. Attention, the other LayerNorms, the earlier
  layers, the embeddings and the tied LM head stayed frozen at the teacher.
  Both reference papers fine-tune the whole pretrained model (Rasch et al.
  2023; Büchel et al. 2025; see `hwa_papers/`). Absolute KL values, crossover
  rates and source rankings may change under whole-model HWA, and so may the
  degree of domain specialization.
- **One training run per source** (D bounds the variance). L3 ran on a
  different platform. Exploratory, model-based evidence.

## Decision

**Settled for recovery sources:**
- Always use a decaying learning rate. The tested form (×0.1 after epoch 40
  of 50) is the minimum.
- Drop mixed-rate and over-estimated (5 %) corruption-aware training, and
  noise above 9 %.

**Pending your decision.** Together with exp-004's domain question, I propose
one in-domain confirmation before Experiment 2. On the chosen domain, with
decay, compare:
- standard 3 % noise;
- standard 9 % noise;
- corruption-aware 3 %.

That is three trainings plus deployments, about 2 GPU-hours. It picks the
recovery source or sources. The likely carry-forward pair is 9 % noise and
corruption-aware 3 %, if in-domain behaviour follows exp-004.
