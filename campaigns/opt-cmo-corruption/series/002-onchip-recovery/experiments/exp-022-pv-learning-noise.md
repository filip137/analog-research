# exp-022: noiseless and noisy learning after intelligent P&V

## Question and authorization

On October 16, 2026 the user asked for two learning results for every case in
[the October 22 meeting summary](../../../../docs/meeting_2026_10_22.md):
- (a) noiseless on-chip learning;
- (b) noisy on-chip learning.

Both start from the intelligent-P&V state. The user delegated the choice of
scheduler and learning rates. Execution is authorized on the Jean Zay V100 dev
queue, as in [exp-021](exp-021-onchip-lr-screen.md).

Question: per fault kind and rate, how much do noiseless and noisy learning add
after intelligent P&V, and how large is the gap between them?

The comparison is descriptive and has no decision rule. The noiseless arm is a
non-physical bound on what this objective and budget can reach with exact writes.

## Frozen comparison

**Cases**, all on array 271002 (one array, by standing instruction), std3 source:
- gmin and gmax at 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6% stuck physical devices: 12 cases. They
  use the exp-016 phase-3 deployment `db378a16…`.
- Healthy and 3% random: 2 cases. They use the exp-013 P0 `3e35ce9b…` (the
  meeting's random-fault rows).

**Arms per case**, with the same deployment and rewrite in both laws:
1. `none`: initial P&V.
2. `pair_rewrite_verify`: intelligent P&V, a 5σ verify detector and partner
   rewrite with seed 241016, unchanged from exp-016.
3. `pair_rewrite_verify_recovery`: the same rewrite, then learning.

**Learning laws.** Recipes are fixed before running and identical across cases:
- **(b) Noisy**, the exp-016 / meeting recipe unchanged:
  - thresholded Adam, accumulated, nominal 2σ threshold;
  - constant LR 3e-3, at most 4 attempts per active device;
  - physical write noise.

  exp-021 retained this recipe: no tested noisy alternative was healthy-safe
  without recovering less at 3%. It also makes the six existing 2/4/6% learning
  endpoints exact cross-host anchors.
- **(a) Noiseless:** exp-021's valid oracle:
  - dense `cmo_idealized_write_adam` with `write_noise_scale: 0`;
  - cosine warm-up/decay at peak 3e-3 (13 warm-up updates, decaying to zero);
  - no attempt cap.

  This was the best and healthy-safe exact recipe at 3% random faults in
  exp-021.

**Common to both:** 256 updates, batch 32, 2,097,152 adaptation tokens,
`teacher_kl` objective, data seed 20261008, on-chip seed 53. Reference devices
and digital parameters stay fixed. The final endpoint is fixed in advance.
Evaluation uses the unchanged development (256), generated (384) and WikiText
(1,024) cohorts on held apparent state. Teacher KL and PPL are reported per case
and never pooled.

**Validity checks:**
- Hold arms, and the rewrite receipts and states, are identical between the two
  laws for every case.
- The noisy 2/4/6% gmin/gmax endpoints are compared with exp-016 phase 4, and the
  noisy random-3% endpoint with exp-016 phase 1 (271002). Differences are
  reported, not gated.
- All bundles pass native inspection and replay.

## Implementation

- **Exact learning after a rewrite.** `onchip.write_noise_scale` now admits
  `fault_rewrite` arms. The rewrite is a P&V programming event: it keeps its
  physical endpoint noise from its own per-matrix streams. The factor applies
  only to learning writes.
- **Test:** `test_exact_write_learning_starts_from_the_same_physical_rewrite`
  checks that a physical and an exact lifecycle produce identical rewrite
  receipts and rewritten states.
- **Schedule:** `onchip.lr_schedule` comes from exp-021.
- **Lifecycles:** generated by `launch/exp22_pv_learning.py generate`, ten in all
  (5 case groups × 2 laws).

### Incident 1: raw deployment not portable to V100 (October 16)

- **What failed:** the eight gmin/gmax jobs (1183485–1183495), at load time,
  before any training: `RuntimeError: The rebuilt CMO deployment differs from
  the saved one.`
- **Cause:** the exp-016 phase-3 deployment `db378a16…` is a raw native bundle.
  Its held state is rebuilt from seeds and checked bitwise. The V100/PyTorch 2.5
  rebuild does not match the RTX 5090/PyTorch 2.11 original. The two random-3%
  jobs use the exp-013 P0, which was already in portable form, and are unaffected.
- **Fix:** convert the deployment to portable form with
  `python -m workflow.portable_deployment` on an idle RTX 5090 (the original
  architecture). This makes no new device writes and checks the replay. Then
  resubmit the same eight lifecycles with that portable state as
  `device_state`. This is the one allowed retry for an understood operational
  failure.
- **Preserved:** the failed attempts and logs stay under
  `$JZ/hwa-c9269c7/results/lifecycles/<id>/runs/` and `$JZ/logs/`.
