# Result: training through the verified partner after rewriting

**Coverage is partial: two of three assignments, 16 of 24 endpoints.** On the
completed assignments, training through the verified healthy partner slightly
worsens WikiText teacher KL compared with rewriting alone and original-active
training. It improves generated-text KL relative to rewriting alone. The frozen
three-array decision is pending; neither completed array will be rerun because
of its numerical outcome.

## Preliminary measurements

Means below use **only assignments 271002 and 271004**, at 3% faults. Lower
teacher KL is better. The generated and WikiText domains are not pooled.

| Arm | Generated teacher KL | WikiText teacher KL | WikiText perplexity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original faulty hold | 0.133048 | 0.188740 | 57.125 |
| Verify partner rewrite, hold | 0.028518 | 0.054263 | 49.776 |
| Rewrite + original-active training | 0.027203 | 0.055341 | 49.569 |
| Rewrite + verified-partner training | 0.027438 | 0.055532 | 49.571 |

Relative to rewrite hold, partner training raises WikiText KL by 0.001252
and 0.001285 on the two arrays. Relative to original-active training it raises
WikiText KL by 0.000265 and 0.000118. Generated KL improves over rewrite hold
on both arrays, but is slightly worse than original-active training on both.
Healthy-array mean WikiText KL rises from 0.043989 after rewriting to 0.050395
after partner training. These are observations on two arrays, not the final
predeclared three-array verdict.

At 3% faults, partner training adds mean **36,526,727 issued write attempts**:
36,448,220 successful device writes, including 1,012,650 reference writes,
and 78,508 attempts on stuck devices. It touches about 20.593M devices and
adds **zero verify reads**, with at most four attempts per selected device.
These costs are additional to the rewrite's full 56,623,104-device scan and
approximately 1.54M programming attempts. Programs and writes are modeled
events, not measured pulse counts; internal P&V pulse/read costs are absent.

## Interpretation and next decision

The two completed arrays do not indicate a WikiText teacher-KL benefit from
opening the reference device to learning after rewriting. The small perplexity
improvement versus rewrite hold is a different metric and does not overturn
the declared teacher-KL comparison. No new recipe or tuning sweep follows from
these partial results. Complete array 271003, validate all 24 endpoints, then
apply the frozen decision separately by fault condition and held-out domain.

Results are exploratory and model-based. They assume independently programmable
per-weight references, fitted CMO endpoint noise and permanent synthetic faults,
with no read noise, drift or physically loaded passive-network evaluation.
This comparison cannot establish general failure of on-chip training.
