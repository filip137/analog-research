# Result: training through the verified partner after rewriting

**Training through the verified healthy partner does not improve WikiText
teacher KL after rewriting in this tested regime.** It slightly worsens KL on
all three assignments relative to both rewriting alone and original-active
training, while adding about 36.7M write attempts. Generated-text KL improves
relative to rewrite hold, but remains worse than original-active training.

The assigned comparison is complete: **three arrays, six starting conditions,
24 endpoints and 12 learning trajectories**. This final result supersedes the
earlier two-array summary. The [experiment note](../experiments/exp-017-partner-training.md)
owns the frozen comparison and decision rule.

## Teacher KL and physical cost

Means below summarize the **three assignments at 3% faults**. Lower teacher KL
is better. Decisions use each assignment separately; domains are not pooled.

| Arm | Generated teacher KL | WikiText teacher KL | WikiText perplexity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original faulty hold | 0.133427 | 0.188471 | 57.119 |
| Verify partner rewrite, hold | 0.028241 | 0.054036 | 49.818 |
| Rewrite + original-active training | 0.027003 | 0.054757 | 49.510 |
| Rewrite + verified-partner training | 0.027207 | 0.054903 | 49.513 |

| Assignment | Rewrite hold: WikiText KL | Original-active training | Verified-partner training |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 271002 | 0.055287 | 0.056274 | 0.056539 |
| 271003 | 0.053580 | 0.053590 | 0.053644 |
| 271004 | 0.053240 | 0.054407 | 0.054525 |

Partner training raises WikiText KL versus rewrite hold by 0.000063–0.001285
and versus original-active training by 0.000053–0.000265. Its mean increase
versus rewrite hold is about 1.6%. On healthy arrays, mean WikiText KL rises
from 0.043927 after rewriting to 0.050158 after partner training; healthy
generated KL also worsens on all three arrays.

| Additional training cost at 3% faults, mean | Original-active | Verified-partner |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Issued write attempts | 36,917,842 | 36,666,462 |
| Successful device writes | 35,774,379 | 36,587,640 |
| Successful reference writes | 0 | 1,018,027 |
| Attempts on stuck devices | 1,143,464 | 78,823 |
| Distinct healthy devices touched | 20,113,668 | 20,635,681 |
| Verify reads | 0 | 0 |

The new routing reduces stuck-device attempts by about 93%, but that does not
translate into better KL. The cap is four attempts per selected physical device
after rewriting. The routing map is fixed from the original verify scan and
occupies 28,311,552 bytes; it does not use the hidden fault map. Detected
both-faulty pairs are blocked; undetected faults remain stuck and consume attempts.

Training costs are **additional to** the shared rewrite: mean 1,533,054
successful device programs plus 3,320 stuck attempts, with one full scan of
**56,623,104 per-device verify reads**. All costs exclude the original deployment.
Programs and writes are modeled events, not measured pulse counts; internal
P&V pulse/read costs are absent.

## Frozen decisions and interpretation

The predeclared sign rule yields these outcomes for verified-partner training:

| Fault condition | Domain | Versus rewrite hold | Versus original-active training |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healthy | Generated | Harms on all three | Mixed |
| Healthy | WikiText | Harms on all three | Mixed |
| Random 3% | Generated | Improves on all three | Harms on all three |
| Random 3% | WikiText | Harms on all three | Harms on all three |

These signs are not significance tests. The small WikiText perplexity improvement
versus rewrite hold is a different metric and does not overturn the declared
teacher-KL comparison. Opening the healthy reference to learning successfully
redirects physical updates and avoids most futile writes, but supplies no
additional teacher-KL benefit over the original-active recipe on faulty arrays.

For this source and recipe at 3% random faults, **rewrite-only remains the best
tested WikiText teacher-KL endpoint**. The assigned implementation and test are
complete; no new recipe, tuning sweep or broader regime was launched. The result
does not establish that all on-chip training after rewriting must fail.

Results are exploratory and model-based. They assume independently programmable
per-weight references, fitted CMO endpoint noise and permanent synthetic faults,
with no read noise, drift or physically loaded passive-network evaluation.
There is one HWA source/training seed, three independent arrays and fixed held-out
cohorts. Broader device regimes and measured hardware costs remain untested here.
