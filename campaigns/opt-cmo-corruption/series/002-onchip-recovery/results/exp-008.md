# Result: exp-008, dense on-chip pilot

The [experiment note](../experiments/exp-008-overnight-recovery.md) owns the
frozen comparison. **The declared full-pass pilot is complete and negative:**
generated teacher KL rose from 0.133780 to 0.379083, and WikiText perplexity
from 57.058 to 102.332, after 167.49 billion healthy-device writes. Both final
trajectories replayed exactly. Readiness evidence is retained below; the full
endpoint, cost and scoped interpretation follow it.

The one-MLP smoke completed on loulou in 17.30 seconds of native campaign time,
20.49 seconds including queue transport and declared validation. It used
4,718,592 analog weights, a pretrained teacher source, 64 training sequences,
two optimizer steps, 3% permanent random faults, and matched hold/learn arms.
Both final states replayed exactly. The learning arm recorded 9,155,042 healthy
active-device writes, 282,142 stuck-write attempts, 4,577,521 touched devices,
maximum two writes per device, and zero verify reads. Peak CUDA reserved memory
was 13,899,923,456 bytes (12.95 GiB). There were 2,347 out-of-range devices at the
endpoint; unclamped CMO write noise permits this and it must remain visible.

## Full-pass endpoint and scoped verdict

The complete 49,977,856-token pass finished on October 12. Both held and learned
states passed native validation and exact saved-state replay. The one declared
recovery assignment and both trajectories are complete; the four extra P0
assignments remain deployment controls, not recovery replicates.

| Metric | Frozen deployment | Dense on-chip, final pass |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Generated held-out teacher KL | 0.133780 | 0.379083 |
| Generated held-out perplexity | 8.166 | 10.569 |
| WikiText teacher KL | 0.189875 | 0.698445 |
| WikiText perplexity | 57.058 | 102.332 |
| Synthetic development KL | 0.136692 | 0.402117 |

The epoch took 1,066.60 seconds, including endpoint evaluation; the complete
learned-arm processing took 1,084.89 seconds. All 195,226 sequences were used
exactly once (6,101 updates). Adaptation issued **167,493,488,910 successful
healthy-device writes**, **5,179,944,610 stuck-write attempts**, touched
27,456,287 active devices, and reached a maximum 6,101 writes/device. No verify
reads or reprogramming calls occurred. Final out-of-range devices: 9,266,970.

**Verdict:** this dense idealized-write recipe at the chosen constant LR 5e-3
fails the pilot's improvement criterion. Its endpoint is worse than leaving the
array unchanged, at substantial write cost. The canary and full endpoint both
worsen; no claim of convergence or benefit from further epochs is justified.
Accumulated write noise is a plausible mechanism, but this experiment does not
separate it from LR/optimizer choice. The many out-of-range devices also mean
the fitted endpoint-noise law is being extrapolated outside its nominal
conductance range; this limits physical interpretation of this uncapped law. It does not establish that all on-chip
adaptation fails. Sparse writes, tuned physical LRs and matched rewrite/digital
calibration controls remain distinct questions.

The user-authorized next comparison is [digital LoRA](../experiments/exp-009-digital-lora.md)
on the same held physical deployment, with separate development-only LR tuning.
No additional physical training runs were automatically dispatched.
