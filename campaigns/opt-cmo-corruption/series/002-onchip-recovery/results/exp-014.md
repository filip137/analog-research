# Partial result: recovery context length after HWA

At October 12, 22:12 Paris, **10 of 15 screen fits** have completed native
validation and local collection: every context under caps two and four, all on
assignment 271001. The five uncapped fits are queued for loulou behind the
earlier recovery-regime study. All confirmation candidates remain held until
the complete screen is collected. No scientific run has failed.

The primary development metric is teacher KL evaluated at fixed context 1,024;
lower is better. Every row starts from the identical HWA deployment, whose KL
is **0.148204**. Recovery presents the same 2,097,152 input tokens and performs
256 updates at each training context.

| Recovery context | Final KL, cap two | Final KL, cap four |

Context **256 is the shortest that meets the frozen 95%-gain rule at both
evaluation contexts**, separately for both capped budgets. At evaluation
context 1,024, it retains 95.039% of the best cap-two gain and 95.514% of the
best cap-four gain. At evaluation context 256, it attains the best gain in
both grids. Its primary KL reductions from HWA-only deployment are 45.12%
and 47.26%, respectively.

The cap-two margin above the 95% threshold is small. This is a provisional
within-assignment finding; the uncapped grid and three independent-array
confirmations remain necessary. Contexts 64 and 128 fail the primary retention
threshold in both capped grids. Context 512 achieves almost all of the best
primary gain, while context 1,024 adds little or no primary improvement over
512 in this assignment. These comparisons do not establish a general optimum.

At context 256, cap two issues 38,782,165 attempts, including 1,264,192 stuck
attempts, and successfully writes 23,067,012 distinct active cells. Cap four
issues 44,669,859 attempts, including 1,582,603 stuck attempts, and successfully
writes 22,731,865 distinct active cells. Maximum attempts per device are two
and four; both use zero verify reads. These are idealized CMO write events,
not measured physical pulses or energy.

The ten compact collection receipts and file hashes were rechecked locally,
their native exports passed the recovery validator, and their reports were
recomputed from the collected measurements. Audit:
`readiness/interim-review-20261012T2212.json` under the experiment evidence root.
Total charged compute, including readiness, is **0.682 GPU-hours**. All ten
full fits took 212–231 seconds each, below the readiness projection.

The current decision is to continue the unchanged queued screen and conditional
confirmation. Automatic selection has not fired; no held-out reporting metric
was used for this interim assessment. Scientific review remains partial.
