# Readiness result: recovery context length after HWA

At October 12, 21:13 Paris, both two-layer context probes and the six-layer
eight-update canary had passed. The first full screen was running; none of the
15 scientific screen fits had yet completed collection. These probes are
operational evidence only. The [experiment note](../experiments/exp-014-context-length.md)
owns the frozen comparison, decision rule and current execution handoff.

The six-layer canary used the pinned standard-3% HWA deployment, 3% permanent
random faults, context 1,024, cap four, and 28,311,552 analog logical weights.
