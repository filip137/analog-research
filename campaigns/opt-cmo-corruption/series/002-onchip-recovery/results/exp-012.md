## Interpretation and decision

Two attempts already retain most of this short reference's development gain,
reducing the observed maximum successful writes from 15 to 2 (7.5 times).
The total-write reduction is much smaller, 12.72%: many cells still receive
one or two writes. Reducing the worst per-cell cost and reducing total writes
are different objectives. One attempt lowers total writes by 41.30% but loses
more of the recovery. These are descriptive results from one array, not
independent-array evidence or significance estimates.

Four attempts retain 99.46% of development recovery while reducing the observed
maximum successful writes from 15 to 4 (3.75 times). Their total healthy-write
reduction is only 0.51%. Thus four is a promising conservative cap, while two
offers a larger maximum-write reduction with a roughly 6% loss of the reference's
development improvement. These are descriptive candidates, not a predeclared
winner or a newly authorized follow-up.

The declared curve is complete. The current evidence does not establish the best
LR, threshold or training duration for any cap, a full-epoch capped result, or
independent-array robustness. Test cohorts were reported but did not select a
checkpoint. Further work should test the 2–4-attempt region across independent
arrays and against matched no-weight-learning controls before claiming physical
viability. No additional run was dispatched by this review.
