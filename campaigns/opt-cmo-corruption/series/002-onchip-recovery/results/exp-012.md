## Preliminary interpretation

Two attempts already retain most of this short reference's development gain,
reducing the observed maximum successful writes from 15 to 2 (7.5 times).
The total-write reduction is much smaller, 12.72%: many cells still receive
one or two writes. Reducing the worst per-cell cost and reducing total writes
are different objectives. One attempt lowers total writes by 41.30% but loses
more of the recovery. These are descriptive results from one array, not
independent-array evidence or significance estimates.

Finish the declared cap curve before selecting a follow-up. The current evidence
does not establish the best LR, threshold or training duration for any cap.
