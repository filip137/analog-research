**Scoped verdict:** LR tuning over this grid does not rescue the dense
idealized-write recipe at 256 updates on the tested array. The original full
pass at 5e-3 also worsened performance, while digital LoRA improved it. These
observations do not establish that all physical adaptation fails. A matched
diagnostic that disables incremental write noise would help separate optimizer
behavior from write-error accumulation before expanding the sweep; this is a
proposed intervention, not a completed or authorized additional experiment.
As in exp-008, the many out-of-range states limit physical interpretation of
the unclamped fitted noise law. Mechanism remains unresolved.

## Operational failure and correction

Incident 12, `storage_observation_failed`, began around 02:00 Paris. The
automatic storage probe looked for a GPU placement on every storage target.
This campaign has remote GPU placements and a prepared local collection root;
the missing local GPU placement raised `StopIteration`, recorded as an empty
error string. The initial manual storage observation admitted two screens, then
became stale. Admission correctly stopped on stale storage, but the observation
bug prevented the remaining screens from running before their 07:30 deadline.

The persistent watchdog remained alive and successfully invoked its bounded
read-only investigator. Investigation `869ef36e7f1e44a092a1355014db25a0`
reported the storage failure and recommended repairing observation before
further dispatch. It had no authority to change code or extend time. Its report
and original incident remain in the durable ledger. The interactive handoff
receipt checked active workers but missed this campaign-level incident and
did not require a successful automatic storage observation. Its `passed` claim
was insufficient; the earlier completion estimate was wrong.

The correction uses the prepared local collector checkout for local storage
probes, preserves remote placements, and includes target, paths, working
directory and exception type in future error evidence. Four new regression
cases reproduced the missing-collector failure before the fix and test success,
missing preparation, a nonlocal fallback rejection and storage I/O failure.
Root dispatcher plus LR-decision checks pass 149 tests; the deployed older
service checkout passes 132 dispatcher tests. Root fix: `0dc61cc`; service fix:
`7c0af1e`. The frozen training source, manifests, completed results, selection
criteria and deadlines are unchanged. Recovery evidence is retained in
`readiness/storage-recovery-{before-restart,after-restart,resolution}.json`.
The restarted service automatically observed all storage roots successfully
(3.619 GB), with a fresh heartbeat, no new incident and unchanged job states,
attempt history, budgets and frozen contracts. No duplicate launch occurred.
That repair did not automatically restart expired experiments. The subsequent
user instruction explicitly reopened the three missing screens and the existing
conditional full fit until 11:30 Paris. The original failed window and incident
remain recorded; no completed computation is repeated and no budget is reset.
