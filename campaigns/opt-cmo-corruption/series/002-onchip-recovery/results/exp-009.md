# Result: exp-009, digital LoRA on the held faulty array

**Partial coverage.** All three development screens completed and passed native
artifact validation, exact saved-state replay, frozen base/array checks and zero
adaptation-write checks. The full-pass endpoints are still pending. The
[experiment note](../experiments/exp-009-digital-lora.md) owns the frozen design.

The comparison uses the standard 3% Gaussian HWA source, permanent 3% random
faults on array 271001, and the final six OPT-125M MLPs (28,311,552 logical analog
weights). Digital adapters correct those same matrices; physical devices and
all base-model parameters remain held. This is a single-array model-based pilot.

## Development-only LR selection

Each LR used 8,192 synthetic sequences, or 2,097,152 input tokens, with a fresh
adapter and optimizer. Selection uses final development teacher KL; test scores
do not enter the decision. Each selected full fit restarts from P0 and uses one
fresh complete pass of the declared 49,977,856-token adaptation cohort.

| Rank | Dev KL, LR 3e-4 | Dev KL, LR 1e-3 | Dev KL, LR 3e-3 | Selected LR |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 0.100352 | 0.093245 | 0.090161 | 3e-3 |
| 16 | 0.092711 | 0.081594 | 0.079612 | 3e-3 |
| 64 | 0.080686 | 0.070121 | 0.076870 | 1e-3 |

Ranks 4 and 16 select the upper tested boundary; these short screens do not
establish an LR optimum for a full epoch. The rates remain frozen for the
declared overnight comparison. Peak reserved CUDA memory was 17.81–17.87 GB,
within the 20,000 MiB reservation. Measured per-rank full-pass projections,
including a 35% training margin and 300 seconds of overhead, are 1,947–1,970
seconds. Queueing and final collection add wall time.

The development jobs charged 235.03, 237.05 and 237.04 GPU-seconds for ranks
4, 16 and 64 respectively, about 0.197 GPU-hours in total. Tuning exposure and
compute are additional to the matched final 50M-token fits. Dense exp-008 used
the user's chosen LR rather than this development grid; compare endpoints with
that optimization asymmetry visible.

controller API accepted timing calibration only on the global `handoff` gate,
while this design supplies separately frozen rank-selection gates. The bounded
read-only investigator reported the collection failure and recommended retaining
the successful computation. The operational fix scopes calibration to each
case's predeclared required gates, preserves immutable receipts and also includes
collection error logs in future investigator snapshots. It changes no scientific
setting. Only collection is retried; the original failed collection evidence is
retained. Root regression suite: 125 passing dispatcher tests. Source and restart
receipts: `readiness/controller-gate-recovery.json` and
`readiness/recovery-{before,after}-restart.json`.

Final per-rank endpoints will be collected automatically into
`reviews/r4-lr3em3-50m.json`, `reviews/r16-lr3em3-50m.json` and
`reviews/r64-lr1em3-50m.json`. Until those validated endpoints exist, the full
comparison has no terminal scientific verdict.
