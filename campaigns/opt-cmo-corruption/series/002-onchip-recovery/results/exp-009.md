# Result: exp-009, digital LoRA on the held faulty array

**Complete declared coverage.** All three development screens and selected
full-pass fits completed and passed native artifact validation, exact saved-state
replay, frozen base/array checks and zero adaptation-write checks. The
[experiment note](../experiments/exp-009-digital-lora.md) owns the frozen design.

The comparison uses the standard 3% Gaussian HWA source, permanent 3% random
faults on array 271001, and the final six OPT-125M MLPs (28,311,552 logical analog
weights). Digital adapters correct those same matrices; physical devices and
all base-model parameters remain held. This is a single-array model-based pilot.

Each fit consumed all 49,977,856 input tokens once and retains its fixed final
endpoint. Reductions use each fit's unchanged same-array control. Array/base
hashes are unchanged in all three fits, with zero
device writes, programs, stuck-write attempts, touched cells or verify reads.
The 321,385 initially out-of-range devices also remain unchanged; that diagnostic
is not an adaptation cost. Successful execution plus native validation charged
1,071.55 seconds on loulou (rank 4), 2,717.43 seconds on the local RTX3090
(rank 16), and 3,060.80 seconds on nom-cool-1's RTX3090 (rank 64). Including
development screens and charged failed attempts, the LoRA queue used 7,604.04
GPU-seconds (2.112 GPU-hours), plus 6.07 GPU-seconds for portable P0 preparation.
Compact collection and revalidation succeeded for all three endpoints.

Digital correction recovers performance on this array, with rank 64 best among
the tested capacities at a larger digital storage and inference cost. This does
not establish a general rank optimum, convergence, an independent-array result,
or on-chip physical-learning viability. The negative dense exp-008 endpoint and
its incomplete LR follow-up remain separate evidence. None of the full LoRA
fits worsened development KL relative to its selected short screen, so all three
development-triggered follow-up decisions are false; no extra LoRA fit ran.
