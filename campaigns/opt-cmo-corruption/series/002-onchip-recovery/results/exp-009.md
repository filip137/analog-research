# Result: exp-009, digital LoRA on the held faulty array

**Partial coverage.** Rank 4 completed the full pass and reduced generated
teacher KL by 37.89%, with zero adaptation writes. All three development screens
completed and passed native artifact validation, exact saved-state replay,
frozen base/array checks and zero adaptation-write checks. Ranks 16 and 64 still
need their full-pass endpoints. The
[experiment note](../experiments/exp-009-digital-lora.md) owns the frozen design.

The comparison uses the standard 3% Gaussian HWA source, permanent 3% random
faults on array 271001, and the final six OPT-125M MLPs (28,311,552 logical analog
weights). Digital adapters correct those same matrices; physical devices and
all base-model parameters remain held. This is a single-array model-based pilot.

## First full-pass endpoint: rank 4

| Metric | Held deployment | Rank-4 LoRA, LR 3e-3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |

| Synthetic development KL | 0.136692 | 0.086470 |

The fit consumed all 49,977,856 input tokens once and retains the fixed final
endpoint. Generated and WikiText KL reductions are 37.89% and 26.61% relative
to the unchanged same-array control. Array/base hashes are unchanged, with zero
device writes, programs, stuck-write attempts, touched cells or verify reads.
The 321,385 initially out-of-range devices also remain unchanged; that diagnostic
is not an adaptation cost. The adapter adds 184,320 digital parameters and the
same number of MACs per token. Execution plus native validation charged 1,071.55
seconds on loulou; compact collection and revalidation succeeded.

This establishes recovery by this digital correction arm for the tested array.
It does not establish a general rank optimum or rescue the negative dense
physical-update recipe. The full comparison remains incomplete until the other
two ranks finish. Evidence: `reviews/r4-lr3em3-50m.json` and the collection
receipt for attempt `4a7a295f32d646c6a45db5d13ec674f0` under the evidence root.

The development jobs charged 235.03, 237.05 and 237.04 GPU-seconds for ranks
4, 16 and 64 respectively, about 0.197 GPU-hours in total. Tuning exposure and
compute are additional to the matched final 50M-token fits. Dense exp-008 used
