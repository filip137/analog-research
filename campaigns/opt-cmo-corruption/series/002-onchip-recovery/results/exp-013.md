# Partial result: recovery across HWA and corruption regimes

Snapshot October 12, 20:24 Europe/Paris: eight of 27 jobs, 48 of 162 recovery
endpoints, have completed native validation and collection. The
[experiment note](../experiments/exp-013-recovery-regimes.md) owns the frozen
comparison and execution handoff. This is incomplete coverage, with no general
conclusion across assignments or automatic recipe selection.

For assignment **271002**, the matched four-attempt comparison is complete across
all six fault rates. These are WikiText teacher KL values; lower is better.
Six analog MLPs contain 28,311,552 logical weights. Recovery uses the frozen
2,097,152-token, LR 0.003, nominal 2-sigma accumulated-write recipe.

All three sources improve their own held WikiText and generated-test baselines
at each tested nonzero fault rate in this assignment. Both standard Gaussian
sources worsen on the healthy condition; fault-aware HWA improves there but
remains worse than the healthy standard-3% HWA-only baseline. Recovery therefore
has a regime-dependent benefit under this fixed recipe.

For the fault-aware source at 3% actual faults, cap 2 reaches WikiText KL
**0.134972**, compared with **0.134892** uncapped, from **0.152026** at deployment.
It retains **99.53%** of uncapped improvement in this assignment. The uncapped
trajectory reaches a maximum of 11 successful writes per active cell; capped
attempts also charge writes aimed at stuck devices. This comparison does not
establish the same retention across all sources, rates or assignments.

The initial outcomes support testing the stated question rather than assuming
that more adaptation always helps. They do not establish an optimized recovery
method: the recipe is fixed, and rewrite/digital-calibration comparisons remain
outside this experiment. Results are exploratory simulations of idealized CMO
writes, not fabricated-hardware measurements.
