# H-011 — Four-device one-pulse gradient thresholds

## Provenance and claim

Imported verbatim from the first linked historical JSON plan. This record was reconstructed on 2026-10-02; it is not a newly preregistered hypothesis. The original plan owns its timing, arms and decision thresholds.

A fixed layer-specific positive-gradient threshold can prevent the destructive saturation seen in the zero-threshold one-pulse-down control while retaining useful decrease-only adaptation: at least one p90, p99, or p99.9 arm will improve selected validation accuracy by at least 5 percentage points over the shared isotonic initialization and keep epoch-10 last-pulse saturation below 50 percent in both layers.
