# Result: exp-006 — Five full recovery epochs with OM and PCM

## Evidence and coverage

32 complete retained native attempts including canaries/revisions. The final four studies have 20 stages, 480 controls and 1,920 post-recovery test endpoints.

The [original report](../../../../../docs/cifar_full_epoch_results.md) contains the detailed metrics, provenance,
plots and exclusions. The [experiment](../experiments/exp-006-record.md) indexes every
associated result root. Historical records remain `imported-summary`; engineering
validation is a separate [audit](../../006-migration/results/exp-010.md).

## Measurements

CIFAR-100 PCM 1% Gmax from standard HWA: calibration gives 61.34% / KL 0.59994; joint recovery gives 66.04% / KL 0.35199. Nominal PCM calibration gives 69.91% / KL 0.02597 while joint recovery gives 68.75% / KL 0.09562.

## Interpretation and limits

OM uses incremental pulse updates; PCM uses Gaussian endpoint reprogramming. CIFAR-10 benefits are mostly fidelity or calibration effects. Comparison with twenty 5,000-image passes also changes cohort, total presentations and array draws.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.

## Decision

Retain as evidence for the campaign question, including negative controls and failed
attempts. No additional training was run during migration. Future scientific planning
belongs here; old instructions to finalize global manifests are historical provenance.
