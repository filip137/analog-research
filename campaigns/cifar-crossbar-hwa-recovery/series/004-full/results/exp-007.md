# Result: exp-007 — Four/eight-convolution fault-rate sweep

## Evidence and coverage

562 complete retained native attempts including earlier revisions and canaries. Final V2 evidence comprises 376 campaign nodes and 6,480 control outcomes, not 6,480 independent arrays. The historical audit recorded 192 selected endpoint replays.

The [original report](../../../../../docs/cifar_fault_rate_sweep_results.md) contains the detailed metrics, provenance,
plots and exclusions. The [experiment](../experiments/exp-007-record.md) indexes every
associated result root. Historical records remain `imported-summary`; engineering
validation is a separate [audit](../../006-migration/results/exp-010.md).

## Measurements

At 5% stuck-high on CIFAR-100, CDT plus joint recovery reaches 60.33% (OM/four convs) and 47.98% (OM/eight), versus matched CDT calibration 40.69% and 21.55%. Against stronger generic noise-HWA plus rewriting/calibration, the gains narrow to 2.94 and 5.80 points. CIFAR-10 OM/eight gains 0.96 points over noise-HWA plus calibration.

## Interpretation and limits

31 of 40 selected HWA fits triggered late-convergence flags under the 60-epoch cap. Weak fault-specific CDT sources inflate within-CDT gains. Three array draws measure hardware spread, not HWA-seed spread. Low/random-fault conditions include losses; this does not establish necessity against optimized HWA.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.

## Decision

Retain as evidence for the campaign question, including negative controls and failed
attempts. No additional training was run during migration. Future scientific planning
belongs here; old instructions to finalize global manifests are historical provenance.
