# Result: exp-008 — One-pulse open-loop recovery and RESET controls

## Evidence and coverage

32 complete native attempts. The final screen contains 5,184 controls across both datasets; each dataset has twelve full runs, 2,592 controls and 1,944 recorded endpoint replays.

The [original report](../../../../../docs/cifar_om_open_loop_results.md) contains the detailed metrics, provenance,
plots and exclusions. The [experiment](../experiments/exp-008-record.md) indexes every
associated result root. Historical records remain `imported-summary`; engineering
validation is a separate [audit](../../006-migration/results/exp-010.md).

## Measurements

CIFAR-10 standard HWA, eight convs, 3% stuck high: open-loop joint recovery reaches 81.25% / KL 0.4690, versus historical closed-loop 75.69% / KL 0.672. CIFAR-100 nominal four-convolution open-loop joint recovery drops to 60.65% / KL 0.7004 while calibration gives 69.27% / KL 0.0877.

## Interpretation and limits

Inherited rates can help severe faults while damaging nominal arrays. The older closed-loop budget was capped and the RESET arm changes initial programming; neither comparison alone identifies controller superiority.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.

## Decision

Retain as evidence for the campaign question, including negative controls and failed
attempts. No additional training was run during migration. Future scientific planning
belongs here; old instructions to finalize global manifests are historical provenance.
