# Result: exp-009 — Development-selected closed-loop learning rates

## Evidence and coverage

24 complete native runs: four canaries, eight development and twelve confirmation runs. 336 full-cohort and 96 smoke trajectories, with 432 historical epoch-five replays.

The [original report](../../../../../docs/cifar_om_closed_loop_lr_results.md) contains the detailed metrics, provenance,
plots and exclusions. The [experiment](../experiments/exp-009-record.md) indexes every
associated result root. Historical records remain `imported-summary`; engineering
validation is a separate [audit](../../006-migration/results/exp-010.md).

## Measurements

All four dataset/depth groups select LR 1e-3. At eight convs/3% stuck high from standard HWA, CIFAR-10 rises from 75.78% / KL 0.6696 to 84.03% / KL 0.3685; CIFAR-100 rises from 30.60% / KL 2.1895 to 41.52% / KL 1.6741.

## Interpretation and limits

Higher LR increases pulse expenditure and worsens nominal CIFAR-10 KL. Eight-convolution 5% stuck-high accuracy remains poor. Historical open-loop comparison is not a fully matched rate-tuned controller ranking; fault-aware HWA sources and other fault kinds were not tuned here.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.

## Decision

Retain as evidence for the campaign question, including negative controls and failed
attempts. No additional training was run during migration. Future scientific planning
belongs here; old instructions to finalize global manifests are historical provenance.
