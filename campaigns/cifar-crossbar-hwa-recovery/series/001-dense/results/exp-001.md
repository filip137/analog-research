## Measurements

On the two held-out arrays, mean healthy accuracy is 49.18% after HWA and 50.66% after closed-loop recovery; faulted accuracy is 42.83% and 49.51%. The original digital teacher scores 50.97%. Closed-loop gains are +1.475 and +6.675 percentage points respectively.

## Interpretation and limits

The selected apparent endpoints average 50.08% across conditions/held-out arrays but hidden persistent accuracy is 29.95%. This is a held-apparent-state model result, not retention robustness. Two arrays and one teacher seed do not establish statistical significance; selected-state rollback would require physical reprogramming.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.

## Decision

Retain as evidence for the campaign question, including negative controls and failed
attempts. No additional training was run during migration. Future scientific planning
belongs here; old instructions to finalize global manifests are historical provenance.
