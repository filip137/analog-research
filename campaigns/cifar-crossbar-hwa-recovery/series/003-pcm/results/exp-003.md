## Measurements

CIFAR-100 at 1% Gmax: deployed KL 3.16625, calibration 1.23896, weight recovery 0.56761; mean accuracy rises from 34.43% to 61.84%. No other regime except CIFAR-100 1% random has a positive mean KL advantage over calibration for a weight-recovery arm.

## Interpretation and limits

No HWA source was trained in this screen. Nominal recovery worsens KL. Recovery uses complete Gaussian endpoint requests, not incremental PCM pulses; 5,000 images is not a full CIFAR epoch.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.

Retain as evidence for the campaign question, including negative controls and failed
