## Measurements

At CIFAR-100 1% Gmax, CDT weight-only recovery reaches KL 0.38372 and 65.987%, compared with calibration KL 0.44359 and 65.040%. The paired gain is 0.05987 nats and 0.947 points. Joint recovery reaches 66.040% but has higher KL (0.41224) than weight-only.

## Interpretation and limits

These are fixed-budget curves, not established convergence. Earlier one-pass studies used other arrays, so use the within-experiment milestones for a matched duration comparison. CIFAR-10 accuracy gains remain small despite KL gains.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.
