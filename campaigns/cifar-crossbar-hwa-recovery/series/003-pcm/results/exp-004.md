# Result: exp-004 — PCM HWA/CDT and one-pass fixed-array recovery

## Evidence and coverage

22 complete and two failed native attempts are preserved. Final comparisons contain 1,260 control measurements. Each dataset attempted 20 HWA candidates: 19 completed and SGD at LR 0.5 was rejected as nonfinite.

The [original report](../../../../../docs/cifar_pcm_hwa_results.md) contains the detailed metrics, provenance,
plots and exclusions. The [experiment](../experiments/exp-004-record.md) indexes every
associated result root. Historical records remain `imported-summary`; engineering
validation is a separate [audit](../../006-migration/results/exp-010.md).

## Measurements

CIFAR-100 at 1% Gmax: CDT plus calibration gives KL 0.5431 and 63.11%; joint weight/calibration recovery gives KL 0.5025 and 64.02%. Both metrics improve on all three matched arrays. Weight-only recovery has no mean advantage over calibration in this condition.

## Interpretation and limits

Several HWA/CDT fits retain convergence flags. CIFAR-10 has no consistent accuracy advantage over the strongest tested non-weight controls. Failed candidates and superseded native attempts are not independent scientific replicates.

These are exploratory simulation results. Digital gradients/optimizer state are
not a demonstration of fully analog backpropagation. Original reports marked
scientific review pending; this import does not supply a user review or a new verdict.

## Decision

Retain as evidence for the campaign question, including negative controls and failed
attempts. No additional training was run during migration. Future scientific planning
belongs here; old instructions to finalize global manifests are historical provenance.
