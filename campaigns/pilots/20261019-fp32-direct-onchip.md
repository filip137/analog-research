# Pilot: on-chip training from a direct FP32 deployment, without HWA

State: assigned and prepared; launch on Jean Zay `qos_gpu-dev`. Authorization: the user
asked on October 19, 2026 for a dev-queue V100 run that deploys the model directly on the
crossbar arrays instead of HWA training and then trains on chip, and approved this plan.

## Question and decision
Can on-chip training replace HWA? Every earlier recovery (exp-013/016/017) started from
frozen std3 HWA. Here the pinned FP32 teacher is programmed directly, the standard verify
pair rewrite is applied, and the frozen exp-017 on-chip recipe runs on top. Expected:
FP32 is much worse than std3 at 3% faults before rewriting (WikiText KL ≈2.9 versus 0.15,
[exp-001](../opt-cmo-corruption/series/001-hwa-robustness/results/exp-001.md)), so
the rewrite and training may close some of that gap. Alternative: endpoint noise on
unhardened FP32 weights dominates, and 256 noisy capped updates recover little.
exp-001 dropped FP32 as an on-chip starting source; this pilot revisits that choice with the rewrite applied first.

## Frozen comparison (user-confirmed October 19)
- OPT-125M, last six MLPs (layers 6–11): 28,311,552 logical weights, 56,623,104 devices.
  Pinned teacher; attention and the other components digital.
- Source: one HWA arm `{"id": "pretrained", "method": "none"}`: the FP32 teacher, unchanged.
- Devices, deployment, P&V and defects: unchanged from exp-016/017 (CMO 2% fit,
  active/reference pair, per-output-channel absmax, tile 256, hierarchical placement,
  permanent random faults 0–5% deployed). A fresh deployment on assignment **271002**
  only, built on the V100. The std3 P0s are not reused.
- On-chip cases: `nominal` and `random_30000ppm`. Arms: `none` (raw hold, the contract's
  required no-write control; evaluation only), `pair_rewrite_verify` (5σ, seed 241016)
  and `pair_rewrite_verify_recovery`. The recovery arm uses the same rewrite, then
  `cmo_thresholded_write_adam`: LR 3e-3, 2σ accumulated threshold, at most 4 attempts per
  device, 256 updates, batch 32, 8,192 sequences / 2,097,152 tokens, seed 53, data seed
  20261008. Final checkpoint fixed; development every 64 steps is diagnostic.
- Evaluation: 256 development / 384 generated / 1,024 WikiText sequences, length 256,
  held apparent state. Primary teacher KL; perplexity reported.
- Decision (descriptive, one array): per condition and domain, the sign of recovery
  minus rewrite-hold KL. Then a cross-run comparison with the exp-017 std3 rows for
  271002 (WikiText KL at 3%: rewrite hold 0.055287, original-active training 0.056274).
  Those rows come from 5090 runs of a different source, so this is not a matched endpoint
  comparison. No significance or general viability claim; LR not retuned for this start.
