# Pilot: on-chip training from a direct FP32 deployment, without HWA

State: complete and reviewed (October 19, 18:06 Paris); nothing queued. Jean Zay `qos_gpu-dev`. Authorization: the user

## Question and decision
Can on-chip training replace HWA? Every earlier recovery (exp-013/016/017) started from
frozen std3 HWA. Here the pinned FP32 teacher is programmed directly, the standard verify
pair rewrite is applied, and the frozen exp-017 on-chip recipe runs on top. Expected:
FP32 is much worse than std3 at 3% faults before rewriting (WikiText KL ≈2.9 versus 0.15,
[exp-001](../opt-cmo-corruption/series/001-hwa-robustness/results/exp-001.md)), so
the rewrite and training may close some of that gap. Alternative: endpoint noise on
unhardened FP32 weights dominates, and 256 noisy capped updates recover little.
exp-001 dropped FP32 as an on-chip starting source; this pilot revisits that choice with the rewrite applied first.

## Interpretation and next decision
**Observation.** On array 271002, rewrite-then-train is worse than rewrite hold in every
domain and both conditions: training harms in all six comparisons. At 3% faults, WikiText
KL rises from 0.0875 to 0.1179, at a cost of about 45M write attempts. Without HWA, the
verify rewrite alone removes 97% of the raw 3% KL (2.96 → 0.0875). It still stays above
std3's rewrite hold (0.0553) and std3's rewrite + training (0.0563). Healthy FP32 deployment
has lower WikiText KL than std3 (0.0264 versus 0.0443). That is consistent with exp-001,
where model-scope HWA shifts the digital parameters away from the teacher.

**Inference (not tested).** The development curve jumps from 0.07 to 0.28 by update 64
and is still falling at update 256, in both conditions. The frozen recipe (LR 3e-3,
tuned for an std3 start, with noisy capped writes) first damages the unhardened FP32
state and has not recovered within 256 updates and the 4-attempt cap. A lower LR, or more
updates, could change the sign. This pilot does not test either. Cross-run comparison with
std3 is confounded by source, deployment realization and GPU.

**Verdict, scoped.** For this recipe, one array and these two conditions, on-chip training
does not replace HWA: FP32 + rewrite + training is the worst tested 3% endpoint except raw hold,
and rewrite alone is better than training on both sources. Exploratory and model-based,
with no significance claim. No follow-up is launched. Possible next decisions for the user:
an LR screen from the FP32 deployment (for example 3e-4 / 1e-3) on a separate development
lifecycle, or stopping FP32-start recovery.
